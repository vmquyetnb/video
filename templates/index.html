Pencil Timelapse Studio

Turn a finished pencil portrait into a vertical video of it being drawn on a blank page.

Simulated effect. The drawing order, strokes, hatching and the moving pencil are generated with deterministic image processing (OpenCV + NumPy). They are a plausible imitation, not a reconstruction of how the artist actually drew the picture. No AI video model or cloud service is used.
