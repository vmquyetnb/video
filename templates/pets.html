Pet diary in pencil

A day in a pet's life, told by the pet. Each moment is drawn in pencil while the pet's thought is written and read aloud; then the drawing comes to life as the pet's camera with a punchline in captions, and paw prints walk on to the next moment.

Made to be watched to the end: a cold open (the day's big moment freezes and turns to pencil: "how did I end up here?"), a day bar that shows how much is left, a "What happens next?" guess with a countdown, and a question for the comments. Use your own pet's photos and clips, or free photos and clips that may be changed and reused (Openverse, Pexels, Pixabay). Do not use other creators' videos.
