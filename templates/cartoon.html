Cartoon stories

"Peeko & Friends": Peeko, Lulu, Kiko and Bobo act out stories for young children in cartoon scenery. They talk to each other in speech bubbles, each in its own voice; children count along, answer picture quizzes and learn one thing per episode. Season 1 (12 episodes) is ready to animate.

Everything is made by the program. The characters are drawn frame by frame, so their arms, legs, eyes and mouths move; the scenery and the music too, free to publish. You can also star your own character from a picture: it is cut out and moved as a whole, like a paper puppet.
