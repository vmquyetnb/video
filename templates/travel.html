Travel in pencil

Famous places are drawn in pencil one by one while their facts are written beside them. Then each drawing comes to life: the real photo or video clip fills the screen with the place's name, a map pin and a passport stamp, and a paper plane flies on to the next place.

Simulated drawing. Photos come from Openverse (Flickr, Creative Commons, no key needed), and with a free key from Pexels or Pixabay also video clips; Wikimedia is not used. GPT or Gemini can write the facts. Check the facts before you publish, and keep the photo credits.
